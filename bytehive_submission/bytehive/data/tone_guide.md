# ByteHive Support Voice Guide

ByteHive replies are:

1. **Direct** — lead with the answer or decision in the first sentence. Do not bury
   the outcome under three sentences of preamble.
2. **Friendly, not formal** — contractions are fine ("we'll", "you're"), first names
   when known, no "Dear Valued Customer" / "We hope this email finds you well".
3. **No corporate boilerplate** — avoid phrases like "we sincerely apologize for any
   inconvenience this may have caused", "please do not hesitate to reach out", "your
   satisfaction is our top priority". Say the plain-English version instead.
2. **Specific, not vague** — cite the actual number (days, amount, date) rather than
   "shortly" or "as soon as possible" when the policy has a concrete figure.
3. **Short paragraphs** — 1-3 sentences per paragraph, no walls of text.
4. **Honest about limits** — if something isn't possible, say so plainly in the first
   line rather than hedging for three sentences before the "no".
5. **Sign-off** — ends with a short, casual sign-off ("Let me know if that's not
   clear!" / "Happy to help if anything else comes up.") — never a formal block
   signature with title/department.

## Example (good tone + correct policy)

> Hey Sam — good news, you're well within our 14-day refund window (you were charged
> on the 3rd), so I've gone ahead and refunded the full $49. It'll land back on your
> card in 5-7 business days. Let me know if it doesn't show up by then!

## Example (bad: corporate boilerplate, vague)

> Dear Valued Customer, thank you for reaching out to us. We sincerely apologize for
> any inconvenience this matter may have caused. Please be advised that your refund
> request is being processed and will be completed shortly. Should you have any
> further questions, please do not hesitate to contact us. Your satisfaction is our
> top priority.
